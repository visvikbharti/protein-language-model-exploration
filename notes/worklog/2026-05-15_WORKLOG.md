- 🎯 **§2.3 headline — the independence assumption is operationally safe for PLM5, breaks (favorably) for en31.**
  - PLM5's 4 mutations are spatially *distributed* (closest pair 14 Å, others 22–50 Å). The 1 pair in the trained regime shows epistasis = +0.04 kcal/mol — indistinguishable from zero. The 5 far pairs are by construction additive in ThermoMPNN-D (the coupled head sees no signal at >10 Å). **The campaign's per-position PSSM scoring assumed independent marginals; for PLM5's mutation geometry, that assumption holds.**
  - en31's 3 mutations are *clustered* (all pairs 8.5–9 Å) and 2 of 3 pairs show strong stabilizing coupling (−0.5 to −0.6 kcal/mol). The additive cumulative ΔΔG predicts en31 ≈ neutral (+0.195), but the coupled prediction gives **net −1.18 stabilizing**. This matches Acharya 2024's measured stability gain — en31's wet-lab signal is *enhanced* by inter-mutation cooperation that pure additive scoring misses.
- 🎯 **Operational implication for the campaign:** PLM5 was selected with intentionally distributed mutations (not clustered like en31), so the additive scoring that drove ranking is fine for PLM5. We did not — and could not have — modeled stabilizing cooperation of the en31 kind in PLM5, because PLM5's geometry precludes it. This is a *design choice* baked into the search, not a methodological blind spot for the recommended candidate.
- 🎯 **Falsifiable corollary for §2.5:** ThermoMPNN-D predicts en31's stability gain is ~half cooperative (epistasis −1.4 kcal/mol) and ~half single-residue (additive +0.2, ≈neutral). If en31 single mutants are tested separately wet-lab, they should be near-WT individually but combine non-additively. PLM5 by contrast should look additive — each single mutant should contribute its predicted apo-ΔΔG roughly independently.
