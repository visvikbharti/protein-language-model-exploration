### §2.5 results — Boltz cannot discriminate H from L

Full data table in `notes/plm/verification-log.md` → "2026-05-18 — single-point Boltz E1449L vs E1449H".

**Headline:**
- Every metric Δ within 1σ of E1449H's σ.
- iPTM: 0.926 ± 0.040 (H) vs 0.912 ± 0.077 (L).
- prot↔DNA-PAM iPTM (functional regime): 0.924 ± 0.030 (H) vs 0.919 ± 0.050 (L) — Δ -0.005, indistinguishable.
- Per-residue pLDDT at 1449 itself: 0.951 (H) vs 0.954 (L) — local fold equivalent.
- **E1449L has ~2× higher variance across diffusion samples** — mean structures equivalent, Boltz commits to H more consistently.

**Interpretation: three methods agree at position 1449.** ESM-2 picked L; LigandMPNN picked L (freq 0.985); Boltz says L ≈ H within noise. Acharya 2024 picked H — almost certainly because the en31 triple was selected as a cooperating unit (G1243T + E1369R + E1449H), and the cooperative gain is what §2.3 ThermoMPNN-D measured (Σ epistasis −1.4 kcal/mol stabilizing). A single E1449H on WT backbone doesn't have that cooperation; nor does single E1449L. They look equivalent in isolation.

**Falsifiable wet-lab claim recorded:** If a head-to-head in-vitro test finds E1449L ≥ E1449H, the PLM consensus was right and Boltz's prediction holds. If E1449H >> E1449L, the gap is in something not captured by single-mutation in-silico (most likely the cooperative effect demonstrated by §2.3).
